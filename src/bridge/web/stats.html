Numbers by day
What each line counts
  • Messages: prompts you typed into a tab (bridge's mail, worker briefs, slash commands, tool results and headless runs left out; a paste counts), plus your dashboard messages, once under each agent they went to. A worker counts under its lead. Locked tabs and workers on another host are not read. Claude Code keeps transcripts 180 days, so these lines cover the last 180 days of logs, and older days blank out as their logs are deleted.
  • Questions: blocked and decision posts by the day you answered: by a button, of those the one the post recommended (My pick), in your own words, or settled in chat with the assistant. Withdrawn or dismissed: closed with no answer. Median hours: from the post to your answer, for the questions answered that day.
  • Answers opened: done and insight posts you opened on the page; recorded since that feature shipped.
  • Asks: asks recorded from your words, verified, and reopened, by the day it happened.
  • PRs: published for review is the review channel's post of one of your PRs, by when it was posted. Opened, merged and closed unmerged are PRs by the GitHub account in any repo, agents' included, read from GitHub at most hourly.
  • Lines of code: additions plus deletions, lockfiles and generated files left out as in the review queue; written is the PRs opened that day at their size now, merged the PRs merged that day.
  • Comments: PR comments the review queue judged yours (not an agent's under the shared account), on PRs it tracks.
  • A line starts on the first day its source has a record; before that it is blank, not zero.
